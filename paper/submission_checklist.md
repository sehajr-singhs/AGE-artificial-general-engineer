## Editorial-format notes (Nature MI style)

- Article length: NMI Articles are typically ~5,000–6,000 words; this draft
  is at the upper bound of the range and can be trimmed at revision.
- Display items: NMI allows up to ~10 for Articles; we are at 15 figures +
  5 tables (an over-long draft) and will consolidate the LCA panel
  (Figures 12--15) at revision if the editor requests it.
- All statistical claims report mean relative error and $R^2$; multi-seed
  claims report mean $\pm$ std over 5 seeds with per-domain and pooled
  Wilcoxon signed-rank tests and Cliff's delta (seed controls data,
  shuffling, and weight init); the evaluation protocol is stated in Methods.
- The paper does not claim clinical/medical relevance; no ethics statement
  required. A safety note for autonomous agents is included in Limitations.
